Noily Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Noily ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed and operated by TCnology. Noily respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Noily, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By using Noily, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Noily does not require you to create an account or provide information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the core features of the application.

Depending on the platform and features you use, certain technical and usage information may be processed by third-party services integrated into Noily.

2. Alarm and App Data

Noily may store information necessary for the application to function, including:

  • Alarm settings and schedules.
  • Selected days for alarms.
  • Challenge settings.
  • Timer settings.
  • Calendar Alarm settings.
  • Streak-related information.
  • Application preferences and settings.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide the features of Noily.

Noily does not use alarm names, exact alarm times, challenge answers, or similar app content for advertising purposes.

Noily does not intentionally send alarm names, exact alarm times, challenge answers, or similar personal app content to Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob as part of its normal operation.

3. Permissions

Noily may request certain device permissions that are necessary to provide its functionality. Depending on the platform and device, these may include permissions related to notifications, alarms, sound, vibration, and background operation.

These permissions are used to allow Noily to schedule and deliver alarms, display notifications, play alarm sounds, and provide other features of the application. Noily does not use these permissions to collect unnecessary personal information.

4. Advertising

The free version of Noily may display advertisements on supported platforms.

On Android, Noily uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information, including device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, according to Google's policies and applicable privacy settings.

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with privacy and consent choices regarding advertising and the processing of information for advertising purposes.

Noily Pro removes advertisements from the application.

At the current time, advertisements are not enabled in the iOS version of Noily.

5. Analytics

Noily uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the application and to improve its features and reliability.

Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Application interactions and events.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Application version.
  • General usage information.

Noily uses analytics events related to actions such as creating alarms, completing challenges, creating Calendar Alarms, opening the Noily Pro screen, and completing a Pro purchase.

Noily is designed to avoid sending unnecessary personal information through these events. For example, Noily does not intentionally send alarm names, user names, exact alarm times, or challenge answers as Analytics event data.

6. Crash Reporting

Noily uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may collect information such as:

  • Device information.
  • Operating system information.
  • Application version.
  • Crash and error information.
  • Technical information related to a crash.

This information is used to improve the stability, performance, and reliability of Noily.

7. Third-Party Services

Noily currently uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — for application usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — for crash and error reporting.
  • Google AdMob — for advertising on supported versions of Noily.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

Noily offers paid features through Noily Pro. Purchases and subscriptions are processed through the applicable platform's official payment system, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Noily does not directly collect or store your payment card number or other payment credentials used to complete purchases through these platforms.

9. Data Retention

Information stored locally by Noily remains on your device according to the application's functionality and your device settings.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob is subject to the respective service's data retention policies.

10. Data Deletion and Privacy Choices

You may remove locally stored Noily data by deleting the relevant information within the application or uninstalling the application, depending on the type of data.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have additional rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information.

You may also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your device and Google settings.

11. Children's Privacy

Noily does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to use its core features.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected in a manner that is not permitted by applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Noily and the services we use. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Links to Other Websites

Noily may provide links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those services.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Noily, the services we use, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
